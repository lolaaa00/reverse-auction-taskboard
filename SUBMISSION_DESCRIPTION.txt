Reverse Auction Taskboard decides task assignment by price. A poster escrows a max budget; each worker places one bid before a hard deadline. Once bidding closes, assignment goes to the lowest bidder, ties broken by earliest submission. The assigned worker submits evidence, judged in one prompt_comparative round against the task's criteria, with validators required to converge on the exact literal token MET, NOT_MET, or UNCLEAR -- never a synonym -- so settlement consumes the same canonical token consensus agreed on. A MET verdict pays that worker exactly their own bid and refunds the poster the rest.

If evidence is judged NOT_MET/UNCLEAR, never submitted, or submitted but never successfully judged within a bounded window, assignment falls through to the next-lowest bidder. No bonding to bid, one bid per worker enforced, no owner, no admin, no pause. An exhausted or bid-free task lets the poster reclaim the full budget.
